Companion to the [architecture and release-readiness review](2026-10-18-rc11-adversarial-review.md).
Fix status and regression evidence are recorded separately in the
[source remediation record](2026-10-18-rc11-remediation.md).
References are repository-relative `path:line` locations in published source
`abfff0529a19adc02cf9f4b93eeef78c645dfa8f`; reviewed application code is unchanged

“Confirmed” describes the code defect and stated witness, not every possible

## High-severity findings

### RC11-01 — Deletion continuation can remove a replacement pier

- **Origin:** R2-1/C2; confirmed on temporary filesystem objects.
- **Code:** `internal/runtimequalify/storage_actions_linux.go:100–156`;
  `internal/pierstore/files_linux.go:206–239`;
  `internal/managed/store.go:661–672`.
- **Trigger:** Interrupt confirmed deletion after persisting `renaming`, before
  rename. Move the original child elsewhere and place another owned pier under
  the unchanged recorded mount. Continue with a new request ID.
- **Failure:** Initial review checks full identity and `Busy`; continuation checks
  only the mount and structural tree safety. The replacement fails full
  `pierstore.Check` but is recursively deleted, including its synthetic Vere lock.
  Restart/journal admission permits the continuation. Replaying the old request
  merely returns its receipt, so the trigger requires explicit continuation.
- **Scope:** Owner-side filesystem change during interruption; accidental
  unrelated-pier loss, not privilege escalation. Fresh deletion has stronger guards.
- **Fix/test:** Bind each destructive phase to the recorded child identity and
  recognized locks. Test substitution before/after rename and during partial
  removal. Preserve unexplained objects when identity can no longer be proved.

### RC11-02 — Incoming handoff retry deletes an unrecorded destination child

- **Origin:** R2-2/C2; confirmed on temporary filesystem objects.
- **Code:** `internal/runtimequalify/handoff_linux.go:162–184`;
  `internal/pierstore/files_linux.go:243–259`;
  `internal/managed/handoff.go:313–345`.
- **Trigger:** Interrupt receipt after mount identity persistence while still
  uninitialized. Put a different pier under that mount and continue the handoff.
- **Failure:** `PrepareMount`/`CheckMount` prove only parent ownership; unconditional
  `os.RemoveAll(q.pierPath())` deletes the unrecorded child before restaging. A
  sentinel and synthetic lock were removed and receipt reached `received`.
- **Scope:** A preexisting unrecorded parent is refused. The vulnerable state is
  the already-recorded parent with an unrecorded or replaced child.
- **Fix/test:** Extract into transfer-bound, identity-recorded staging and publish
  without replacement. Retry may clean only positively identified staging data.
  Test interruption both before recording staging identity and during extraction;
  an unexplained child must survive.

### RC11-03 — Desktop move draft can target another runner

- **Origin:** R7-1/C7; extracted-JavaScript witness and real store admission.
- **Code:** `plugins/omabit/SetupPane.qml:174–191`,
  `StoragePane.qml:25,54,109–117`, `Service.qml:447–451`;
  `backend/cmd/urbit-collector/piers.go:69–97`.
- **Trigger:** Runners A and B each have stopped `lab` at revision 1. Open Move for
  A in Add a ship → Existing pier; enter a destination; change the outer selector
  to B; submit the retained draft.
- **Failure:** `onConnectionChanged` clears `selected`, not `moving`. The pane is
  retained; command construction combines B's current pin with A's copied
  ID/revision. The backend correctly authenticates B and can accept a move of
  B's `lab`. The critic demonstrated acceptance in B's temporary journal.
- **Scope:** No mapped desktop or live pier move was executed. QML ownership,
  command construction and backend admission establish the path.
- **Fix/test:** Capture immutable runner/instance/revision/source identity in the
  draft, invalidate on selection change and revalidate before dispatch. Two-runner
  same-name/revision tests must emit zero mutations after target changes.

### RC11-04 — Never-dispatched maintenance blocks host-wide admission

- **Origin:** R1-1/C1; runtime witness plus persisted-state reload/admission tests.
- **Code:** `internal/runtimequalify/docker_linux.go:384–442,569–573`;
  `internal/runtimequalify/managed_linux.go:364–381`;
  `internal/managed/store.go:874–886`.
- **Trigger:** Interrupt maintenance after recording a container intent but before
  `CreateDispatched=true`. No daemon container exists. Request Stop/reconciliation.
- **Failure:** Stop inspects the nonexistent container and cannot resolve the
  record. Retained maintenance blocks unrelated starts and gated work. Restart
  and repeated Stop preserve the blockage.
- **Fix/test:** Use the existing `cleanupOne` proven-undispatched distinction in
  managed cleanup. Reconcile maintenance quarantine from phase evidence too;
  removing the container record alone may leave that fence. Test initial intent
  interruption, reload, cleanup, and subsequent admission on another ship.

### RC11-05 — Update idle check races with accepted maintenance

- **Origin:** R5-1/C5; installer witness plus independent worker-cancellation tests.
- **Code:** `internal/hostinstall/owner.go:262–273,357–365,439–442`;
  `internal/managed/server.go:154–159,242–249`;
  `internal/managed/routines.go:196–229`.
- **Trigger:** Installer observes idle; API or scheduler admits maintenance before
  the eventual management stop. The installer lock does not close runner admission.
- **Failure:** Management shutdown cancels workers before waiting. Accepted work
  can lose outcome tracking and retain a recovery fence despite the earlier idle
  check. The daemon-side utility may continue after its client is canceled.
- **Scope:** Persistent recovery blockage is supported; the witnesses do not
  establish that update kills Vere or corrupts a pier. Actual systemd update was
  not run during review.
- **Fix/test:** Runner-owned quiescence must atomically close API and scheduler
  admission, then drain or refuse while management remains available. Hold it
  through activation. Inject operator/scheduler work at every update boundary;
  another snapshot alone cannot close the race.

### RC11-06 — Subordinate-ID allocation omits NSS-only identities

- **Origin:** R5-2/C5; conditional host-security defect.
- **Code:** `internal/hostinstall/owner.go:114–171`;
  `internal/hostsetup/owner.go:12–44`, `metadata.go:45–77`.
- **Trigger:** Selected owner is local; another user/group is supplied by
  LDAP/SSSD or other NSS source, with an ID inside the proposed subordinate range.
  Subordinate-ID delegation itself is file-backed.
- **Failure:** Allocation and validation inventory only `/etc/passwd` and
  `/etc/group`. The first candidate `1048576–1114111` can include another host
  identity such as `1048577`. Granting that mapping crosses Unix ownership
  boundaries where namespace configuration and filesystem permissions allow it.
- **Evidence limit:** Fixture established the omitted inventory and accepted
  overlapping range; upstream Shadow implementation supports the delegation
  consequence. No NSS provider, mapping helper or cross-user read was exercised.
  A separate sub-ID NSS plugin may reject file-based updates.
- **Fix/test:** Allocate only from an authoritatively reserved range outside all
  identity domains. Refuse automatic allocation when that cannot be established.
  `getent` enumeration alone is not proof on non-enumerating NSS installations.

### RC11-07 — Bridge-local reconnect loses runner identity binding

- **Origin:** R3-1/C3; reduced from High after reachability review.
- **Code:** `internal/access/bridge.go:136–175`, `handoff.go:23–66`;
  `internal/client/client.go:48–64`.
- **Trigger/failure:** A retained SSH stream is pinned to A; after the latest Hello
  check, the private local listener is replaced with B. The bridge reconnects for
  each RPC and checks actor/role/protocol but not the previously presented runner
  ID/mode. A real temporary managed B accepted an attributed Stop: A had zero
  matching receipts, B had one.

  Bulk forwarding shares the missing check; wrong-runner bulk completion was not
  established and signatures/fences still apply.
- **Fix/test:** Pin ID/mode across every inner handshake before forwarding any
  request/body. Test replacement ID and mode with zero mutations on B; preserve
  reconnection to the same identity.

## Implementation and qualification gaps

### G01 — Destination publication is not explicitly durable before source removal

**Potential High impact; power-loss data loss was not reproduced.**
`internal/runtimequalify/storage_actions_linux.go:338–383` copies and verifies a
cross-filesystem destination, removes the held source, then syncs the source
parent. `internal/piercopy/copy_linux.go:279–326` syncs copied files/directories
through the destination root, not that root's containing directory.
`internal/pierstore/files_linux.go:254–259` also creates the enclosing mount without
syncing its parent. The EXDEV rename failure supplies no destination sync barrier.

The missing explicit ordering is established by code. The review witness injects
EXDEV; it does not instrument fsync or simulate power loss. Filesystem-specific
loss frequency/consequences remain unqualified. Sync all newly created destination
namespace entries before source deletion, retain source on barrier failure, then
test sync ordering/failure and controlled crash behavior on supported filesystems.

### G02 — Stable upgrade/rollback contract is not yet qualified

**Existing acknowledged stable-release gate.** After management stop and binary
replacement, failed activation leaves the replacement installed and management
unavailable (`internal/hostinstall/owner.go:439–474`, `host.go:148–166`). The fixture
demonstrates file/command ordering under injected activation failure, not pier
loss. No retained deployment generation or durable activation/recovery journal
provides compatibility-aware rollback. Downgrade schema refusal protects data,
but occurs too late to preserve working management activation by itself.

The schema11–18 preservation sweep constructs current objects and relabels
`user_version` (`internal/managed/upgrade_preservation_test.go:15–55`). Other tests
meaningfully remove legacy fields and exercise rollback/defaults; it would be
