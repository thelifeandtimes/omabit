# All successor identities require unchanged exact processes

The pure readiness collector brackets every replacement's selected published
identity endpoint with fresh complete observations of the successor project.
It retains all piers, exact processes (including the last created replacement),
anchor history and the unchanged resource generation. Ship identities, creator
operations, member generations, endpoints and observation times are retained.

Wrong/missing identities, a restarted first or last process, changed anchor exit
history/namespace/publication, replaced piers, stale/incomplete observations and
cancelled/failed reads refuse. Driver and result copies cannot rewrite authority.
The resulting binding is explicit successor history; it does not mutate original
AnchorStart or grant permission to release a persistent recovery fence.
