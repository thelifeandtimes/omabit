resolves subsequent ordinary member replacements by exact predecessor bindings.
It retains the successor namespace/resource generation, exact initialized piers,
member creators/generations and process starts. Historical prefix lookup validates
the entire supplied suffix before returning an earlier binding. Input order does
not choose a branch or hide unfinished work.

Tests cover three later replacements across both members, every retained prefix,
unchanged prior history, ambiguous/missing/unfinished successors, changed creator,
generation or process, early Stop and reused operation/container identities. Each
bad completed receipt independently validates before the cross-history refusal.

real post-recovery ordinary Docker contracts nor persists or dispatches them.
